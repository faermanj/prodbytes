Hello, and welcome to Delivering Projects on AWS! In this series we're going to practice how to build and deliver software using [Amazon Web Services](https://aws.amazon.com/). Amazon is a pioneer in cloud computing, and many companies use their services to build and deliver software — so there is a good chance the skills we practice here map directly to a project near you.

## Dev side and op side

We're going to cover both sides of delivery: from components such as databases and functions, up to observability and monitoring, and even AI and machine learning — and all the things in between. Everything you need to build and deliver your project in a way that is secure, reliable, and [well-architected](https://aws.amazon.com/architecture/well-architected/).

## Practicing with real projects

My goal is for you to be successful with your project. And for that, we're going to work through several realistic and practical application projects that you can use to build confidence and do your own deliveries — not just watch, but build along.

I'm Julio, and I worked at AWS for many years, and at Amazon Partners as well. Now, on my own consulting and education practice, I build projects with you.

Let's go — and let me know in the comments: what are you delivering on AWS, and where would you like this series to start?
