To begin, I want to make it very clear why we're using [AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/), what we're going to build, and how we're going to do it.

## Why AWS?

There are many reasons to use AWS. It may come from the cost savings, from accelerated building — in general, these are services to help you deliver your software, deliver your apps. And not only hosting in the traditional sense, like web hosting, but also databases, machine learning, caches, message systems, logs — everything you need in the delivery process. Which of these matter most will change with your company size, your interests, and so on.

From my perspective, the best point — my favorite — is democratizing technology access. All those services are available at roughly the same cost to everybody, and anyone can use them. It used to be the case that you needed a hardware provider, long-term contracts, and a lot of money to get this level of infrastructure. Now we can just create an account and start using AWS products and services.

## What we're going to build?

Under [aws.amazon.com](https://aws.amazon.com/) you can find everything we'll be talking about — hundreds of services across a whole range of categories. We're going to use those to build applications and projects. For example, the compute services: [EC2](https://aws.amazon.com/ec2/) for servers, and for containers [ECS](https://aws.amazon.com/ecs/), [EKS](https://aws.amazon.com/eks/), and others — we'll see the differences and compare them as we go. Databases are also very important: we're going to use a lot of them, especially [Aurora](https://aws.amazon.com/rds/aurora/).

The idea is to build practical projects — applications, integrations, whatever you want to build. And that last part matters: comment and let me know what you want to build next.

## How we're gonna do it?

The way I want us to do this is together. We have an organization on [GitHub](https://github.com/prodbytes), we have comments, we have social media — we have everything we need to build together. So let's exchange ideas, exchange code, and I can certainly help you get your first ticket and commit your first lines of code.

Because what I want is for you to see your work in action — see your work in production — and become more comfortable delivering your projects.

So: why are you looking at AWS, and what would you like us to build first? Let me know in the comments — see you in the next one!
