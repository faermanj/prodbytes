Why join ProdBytes?

2026-07-03

Welcome to YOUR software practice! In this video I talk about how you can leverage this substack to help you in your software journey.

There’s plenty of software for US to build

I’ve been an enterprise software engineer for more than twenty years, at companies like Red Hat, Amazon, and Borland, and inside huge organizations in government, telecom, and finance. When you get into those environments, you see that software is not as simple as it looks from the outside. Even with AI, and with how easy it is to start these days, it is still hard to integrate, to operate, and to deliver technology the way modern companies expect.

That is what we are going to work on here: the platforms, languages, libraries, frameworks, techniques, and processes of software engineering. And more than building — getting into the organization, causing impact across teams, measuring that performance, and communicating the results. All of it is part of the journey.

And there is real demand for it. Open LinkedIn or any job board and you will find plenty of opportunities waiting for people who can deliver — roles that stay unfilled because this kind of engineering is genuinely hard to hire for. Those positions are there for us to fill, and you can count on me to help you prepare for them.

Powered by AI, but way beyond it

Everything here will be heavily powered by AI, but we are going way beyond it. I am neither a skeptic nor a hypist: I want to put these tools to use and see, together with you, how intelligent they actually are and how helpful they can be to real systems.

I also want this to be open, direct, and affordable — practical enough that you can get your hands on it, build along, and see everything I talk about in action.

Practicing together

For those who want to take deeper dives, there will be a members area: the projects, the meetings, the automations — a place where we can work together, while the knowledge and the lessons stay open to everybody.

That is how I see this going forward. I hope it is helpful to you. Don’t forget to subscribe — and let me know in the comments: what would you like to practice? What is the next step for you?
